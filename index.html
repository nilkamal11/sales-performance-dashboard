Customer intelligence

Reorder risk and next-best products

The reorder model estimates whether an active customer will record a positive purchase within 60 days using recency, recent documents, active months, revenue trend, product breadth, and credit-note share. Recommendations come from products bought by similar peer customers. Neither measure is a payment-default score or clinical recommendation.

Action queue

Accounts ranked by revenue at risk

Revenue at risk = net sales in the previous 180 days × (1 − predicted 60-day reorder probability). It ranks customers that combine meaningful recent sales with a lower modelled chance of ordering again soon. Example: KES 10M of six-month sales and a 30% reorder probability produces KES 7M of model-weighted revenue at risk. This is a prioritization score—not an unpaid balance, expected accounting loss, credit-default probability, bad-debt estimate, or prediction that all displayed revenue will disappear.

Working capital

Credit-cycle exposure scenarios

This is scenario analysis, not actual receivables aging. The workbooks include invoice dates but no payment dates, receipts, due dates, open balances, or agreed terms. For a selected cycle, exposure is net invoiced sales during the preceding number of days, assuming those invoices remain unpaid for the entire cycle. Customer names containing “CASH” are excluded. Use an accounts-receivable ledger to calculate actual DSO, overdue balances, and collection performance.

Estimate how a 30–180 day collection cycle could change the amount of sales tied up in receivables.

What is known: invoice dates and net sales. What is not known: whether an invoice was paid, when it was paid, its contractual due date, or its remaining balance.

Data needed for actual collection-cycle measurement

With invoice-to-payment matching, the dashboard could report each customer's weighted average days to pay, percentage paid after terms, actual open balance, overdue aging, and collection trend. DSO can then be calculated from average accounts receivable divided by credit sales over the measurement period.

Provide an accounts-receivable export containing customer, invoice number, invoice date, invoice amount, agreed credit term or due date, receipt date, receipt amount, allocation to invoice, and current open balance. That would replace these scenarios with verified days-to-pay and overdue aging.

Credit-cycle scenarios

For each selected cycle, the dashboard sums non-cash-labelled net sales dated within that many days before 31 Aug 2026. This estimates potential working capital tied up only if those invoices remain unpaid for the assumed period. It is not actual DSO or accounts-receivable aging.

Important limitations

Currency is labelled KES from the stated Kenya business context; the source workbooks do not contain a currency field. Customer identity is based on source names and may include aliases. Forecast uncertainty does not include supply disruptions, price changes, tenders, regulatory events, or product launches not present in history.
